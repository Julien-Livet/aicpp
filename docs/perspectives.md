# Perspectives

The underlying approach is not inherently specific to ARC.

aicpp explores a more general paradigm:

    neural guidance
          ↓
    structured program space
          ↓
    executable solution
          ↓
    verification

Potential application domains include:

## Robotics

Trajectory planning through an executable trajectory DSL.

## CAD

Structured generation and transformation of geometric objects.

## Autonomous systems

Planning under explicit constraints.

## Mathematical reasoning

Generation of executable symbolic procedures.

## Structured language

Programs or structured representations as an intermediate
reasoning space.

These applications remain future research directions and are not
presented as demonstrated capabilities of the current system.
